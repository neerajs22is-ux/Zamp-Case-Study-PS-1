## Executive Summary

**Zamp** (`zamp.ai`) is an enterprise-grade agentic AI platform engineering autonomous **digital employees** that execute complex, multi-system operational workflows across finance, accounts payable, procurement, and vendor management. Founded by **Amit Jain** (ex-MD Peak XV/Sequoia India, ex-Head of APAC at Uber, Stanford MBA, IIT Delhi) and **Raghav Saraf** (high school prodigy who skipped college to co-found), the company has raised **\$22M–\$25M** led by **Peak XV Partners** (Sequoia India) with follow-on growth capital from **Valor Equity Partners** and an elite angel syndicate including the CEOs of Uber and DoorDash.

Zamp differentiates from conventional conversational bots and single-app copilots by deploying goal-oriented digital employees that run on **Agent Operating Procedures (AOPs)**, operate directly inside enterprise ERPs (NetSuite, SAP, Workday), self-validate through a **Plan-Execute-Validate (PEV) loop**, and enforce strict enterprise governance (SOC 1/2 Type II, Segregation of Duties, immutable audit trails).

**Institutional Assessment / Strategic Fit for Case Study:** **Exceptional / High Moat.** The take-home case study directly tests the core capabilities of their flagship accounts payable and vendor compliance digital workers.

## 1. Business Overview

### 1.1 Company Description
Zamp was founded on the thesis of becoming a **"Humanity Catalyst"**—eliminating the civilizational bottleneck where millions of skilled workers act as "human duct tape" between fragmented enterprise systems. Rather than building another conversational assistant that requires continuous human steering, Zamp builds autonomous workers that own end-to-end job responsibilities.

### 1.2 Business Model & Monetization
* **Delivery Model:** B2B Enterprise SaaS / Work-as-a-Service (WaaS).
* **Pricing Metric:** Hybrid platform fee + volume-based pricing per digital employee / processed transaction volume (invoices processed, vendors onboarded, accounts reconciled).
* **Value Proposition:** 70–85% reduction in manual processing cycle times, 99%+ extraction accuracy, zero unauthorized payment leakage, and complete elimination of routine AP backlogs.

| Revenue Stream | Model | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise Platform Subscription** | Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | Platform access, Agent Operating Procedure (AOP) environment, Company Brain repository, ERP connectors. |
| **Digital Employee Usage Tiers** | Consumption / Capacity-based | Scaled by transaction volume (e.g., number of monthly processed invoices or vendor validations). |
| **Solutions & Custom Implementation** | Forward-Deployed Eng Fee | Tailoring custom AOPs, complex enterprise integrations, and custom ERP workflow mapping. |

### 1.3 Value Chain Position
```
[Unstructured Inputs]           [Zamp Pace Platform]            [Enterprise Core Systems]
• Supplier PDF Invoices   ───►  • Multi-Modal OCR/Vision  ───►  • ERPs (NetSuite, SAP, Workday)
• Vendor Registration W-9       • Agent Operating Procedures    • Treasury & Banking Rails
• Email Inboxes / Portals       • Plan-Execute-Validate Loop    • Procurement (Coupa)
                                • Human-in-the-Loop Gateway     • BI & Financial Reporting
```
*Zamp acts as the intelligent autonomous execution layer positioned between unstructured customer/vendor communication channels and core enterprise record-keeping software.*

### 1.4 Flagship Products & Capabilities
1. **Pace Accounts Payable Worker:** Ingests invoices, conducts 2-way/3-way matching against POs and Goods Receipts, applies tolerance rules, flags exceptions, and generates ERP vouchers.
2. **Pace Vendor Master Data Worker:** Manages supplier onboarding, validates tax IDs (W-9, GSTIN, PAN), cross-verifies bank account names against legal entities to prevent BEC payment fraud, and automates vendor deficiency notices.
3. **Pace Financial Reconciliation Worker:** Auto-matches bank feeds against ERP general ledgers, drafting reclassification journal entries.

## 2. Market Analysis

### 2.1 Industry Overview & Market Sizing (Enterprise AI Agents / Automation)
The convergence of Large Multimodal Models (LMMs), structured tool use, and enterprise RPA has created the **"Digital Employee"** category, transitioning legacy rule-based RPA into cognitive, adaptive workflows.

| Market Segment | Global Size (2025–2026) | Projected CAGR | Primary Drivers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TAM (Global Enterprise Process Automation & AI Agents)** | **\$38.5 Billion** | **32.4%** | Enterprise demand to replace brittle legacy RPA with adaptive LLM agents. |
| **SAM (Finance & AP Automation Software)** | **\$12.2 Billion** | **24.1%** | Acute shortages of accounting staff, high error rates in manual AP data entry. |
| **SOM (Mid-Market to High-Growth Enterprise Digital AP/Ops Workers)** | **\$1.8 Billion** | **41.0%** | Multi-entity tech platforms, fast-scaling digital marketplaces (e.g., DoorDash, Uber). |

### 2.2 Key Industry Trends
1. **Death of Brittle RPA:** Legacy bots (UiPath, Automation Anywhere) fail when web elements or document formats shift. Modern agents interpret intent and adjust dynamically.
2. **Shift to Job-Centric AI:** Enterprise buyers no longer want general chat interfaces; they want specialized "AI workers" with measurable KPIs and service-level agreements (SLAs).
3. **Zero-Trust Governance & Auditability:** Enterprise CFOs demand immutable audit trails and strict segregation of duties—AI agents can propose and prepare, but human sign-offs remain mandatory on disbursements.

* **Shared Enterprise Memory ("Company Brain"):** Human feedback on a single exception permanently upgrades the collective reasoning across the entire digital workforce.

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk Category | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy Observed in Zamp Architecture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial Hallucination Risk** | High | Critical | The **Plan-Execute-Validate (PEV) loop** prevents LLMs from writing directly to databases without strict tolerance checks and programmatic schema validation. |
| **Vendor Fraud / BEC Attack** | High | Critical | Strict **Segregation of Duties**: AI employees can cross-reference bank data, but any changes to remit-to routing details trigger mandatory human dual-authorization. |
| **Customer ERP Integration Friction** | High | High | Deployment of dedicated **AI Solutions Analysts** (Founder's Office) to manage mapping, custom schemas, and edge-case exceptions on site. |
