# First-time user observations

Walkthrough: authenticated local evaluation workspace, Controller role, 4 November 2026. Navigation and read-only inspection only; no invoice decision, assignment, import, invitation or record change was submitted. The workspace identifies its procurement data as synthetic evaluation data. Examples below omit names, identifiers and amounts. “Might understand” and “confusing” are reasoned novice impressions, not user-test findings. Evidence refers to the current screen and source; screenshots were not saved.

| Area / evidence inspected | Visible and a novice might understand | Confusing or missing |
|---|---|---|
| Shell — sidebar, header and Controller navigation; `frontend/src/components/Sidebar.tsx` | Named sections, help and theme control give several ways to move around; the banner says sign-off does not send bank payments. | “AP”, “sign-off”, “disbursements”, “Exceptions”, “Audit Trail” and the separate showcase tour are undefined at first contact. The sidebar scroll makes some management destinations initially less visible. Other roles' live navigation was not inspected in this walkthrough. |
| Today — populated cards and exception list; `frontend/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx` | A number and a link make work needing attention visible. The metric that cannot be calculated says “Unavailable”. | “Touchless”, “API-defined”, “ERP posting”, “liability”, “exposure” and MSME need plain-language definitions. One card shows a ready-for-approval count alongside a different pending-second-approval count, yet its link opens the latter; explain the distinction and destination. An exception exposure differs from the invoice's billed total without an explanation of the amounts. |
| Inbox — populated list, tabs and period controls; `frontend/src/pages/InboxPage.tsx` | A list has supplier, amount, status and Review; the tabs suggest ways to find work. | “Waiting on Evidence”, “Approved & Cleared”, fiscal year, GSTIN and linked PO are unexplained; “cleared” can sound like paid. Search scope and why a field says “None” or “Not Found” are unclear. Upload and Gmail sync consequences were not exercised. |
| Invoice review — one blocked invoice; `frontend/src/pages/InvoiceDetailPage.tsx` | The heading says approval is blocked and names two failed checks; the document area offers open and refresh. | The embedded PDF remained blank, so source evidence was not visually inspected. “5/7 Gates”, QC, PO, tolerance and variance are unexplained; the action footer uses “4-way match” without saying what a novice should do first. Other action dialogs and successful approvals were not submitted or fully inspected. |
| Reconciliation — same invoice's matched line and procurement flow; `frontend/src/components/ProcurementVerificationFlow.tsx` | It compares invoice, order, receipt and inspection and shows billed versus ordered quantity/rate and a variance. | “GRN”, “QC”, “base”, “debit note”, delta and tolerance need definitions and a plain explanation of why the numbers matter. The view refers to both three-way and four-way matching. Do not hide the compared values or failed checks when simplifying. |
| Exceptions — populated queue with unassigned records; `frontend/src/pages/ExceptionsPage.tsx` | Issue, amount, owner and Resolve make a task list visible; unavailable deadlines are labeled. | “Exception”, “Core Discrepancies”, “Gate”, “Exposure” and “Statutory Deadline” do not explain what is wrong, what amount is measured or what Resolve opens. The relationship between this record queue and the inbox's invoice queue is unclear. Reassignment was not submitted. |
| Approvals — Controller, empty pending queue and two records indicated by the All tab; `frontend/src/pages/ApprovalsPage.tsx` | The independent second-person rule and statement that sign-off is internal help establish the boundary. | “Controller”, “dual approval”, “eligibility” and “Signed Off” need definitions and consequences. No real pending second approval was available, so its enabled, blocked and confirmation states are unverified in this walkthrough. |
| Audit — populated activity table; `frontend/src/pages/AuditPage.tsx` | A recent event list has time, reason, actor and filters; the header explicitly limits this view to 100 recent events. | “Actor”, “correlation”, “state transition”, provenance and CSV are technical; a wide row can require horizontal scrolling to find Details. The view is not a complete ledger. Export and records beyond this window were not tested. |
| Connections — status cards, mailbox and ERP sections; `frontend/src/pages/ConnectionsPage.tsx` | The mailbox is shown as not configured; the procurement section says live ERP sync is not configured and links to records. | “Ingestion”, “SHA-256”, “RBAC”, “OData”, “snapshot” and “vault” obscure which service a finance user needs and what the buttons do. The initial loading view displayed configuration placeholders before the status response; failure states and integration actions were not exercised. |
| Procurement / reference records — populated vendor list and source banner; `frontend/src/pages/SourceRecordsPage.tsx` | The banner identifies evaluation data and read-only records; tabs distinguish vendors, orders, receipts and inspections. | “Provenance”, “PAN”, “MSME”, “authorized”, “remaining balance” and the machine-readable source label need context. Explain whether the numbers are current, and how this snapshot affects an invoice; do not imply live synchronization. Other record tabs and a detail drawer were not exhaustively inspected. |
| Team & Access — Controller roster; `frontend/src/pages/AccessManagementPage.tsx` | Members have roles and brief responsibilities; role-permissions and access-request controls are discoverable. | “Governance tier”, “dual-control authority”, “AP lead” and the threshold need context; personal identifiers dominate space. Other roles' actual permissions and invitation/request outcomes were not tested. |
| Setup — welcome and final checklist steps; `frontend/src/pages/OnboardingPage.tsx` | A seven-step guide can be skipped; final checklist shows what is verified or not confirmed. | “You're Ready to Operate” can overstate readiness when procurement is not confirmed. “Deterministic”, “immutable”, “core invariant” and raw route names are unfamiliar. Intermediate steps and connection/invitation/upload actions were not completed. |
| Welcome — reopened from Help; `frontend/src/components/onboarding/WelcomeModal.tsx` | Shows a five-stage flow, offers an optional tour and warns that it may show live data without submitting decisions. | The first explanation uses “procurement reconciliation”, “governance”, GSTIN, PO, GRN and QC before defining them. The two ways to skip need clearer persistence expectations. |
| Guided tour — started from welcome; `frontend/src/context/TourContext.tsx`, `frontend/src/components/onboarding/GuidedTour.tsx` | It is optional, can be exited and restarted, and says invoice review may show live records. Today has a highlighted target and next control. | It uses “API-defined”, “touchless” and “pending second approvals” without teaching basics. While Today was loading it briefly said the section was unavailable. This walkthrough inspected the start, not every step or the no-invoice/failure paths; their current outcomes are unverified here. |
| Contextual help — Setup and Team; `frontend/src/components/onboarding/ContextualHelpDrawer.tsx` | Screen-specific guidance, terminology, FAQs and restart/welcome controls are present. | Help is behind a separate control; its recommendations still assume familiarity with access and finance terms. In-context definitions and action consequences are not always next to the unfamiliar control. Other help tabs were not exhaustively inspected. |

Visual verification: desktop light was inspected across the listed areas; dark was inspected on procurement, team, setup, welcome and help. Narrow screens, all error/loading states and all roles are unverified in this walkthrough. The embedded PDF is a known visible blocker; opening the document separately was not tested here.
