# Phase 1 — screenshot analysis

**Scope and method.** Every PNG in the seven roots below was opened individually. Each row records the visible pattern and AP opportunity; exact duplicate bytes are still listed separately, with the first matching file identified. “S” means observed in the image; research findings are separately labeled R. Filenames do not establish behavior. AP screenshots are historical captures; current source was inspected separately, and no fresh authenticated runtime was verified. The register covers 206 images across 7 roots.

**Inventory:** 206 PNG files; 133 distinct SHA-256 images. Roots: `competitor_research/01_finbooks/screenshots`, `competitor_research/02_rossum/screenshots`, `competitor_research/03_flickai/screenshots`, `competitor_research/04_cross_product_analysis/screenshots`, `competitor_research/07_phase_a_implementation/screenshots`, `docs/ux-reference-library`, `docs/images/browser_verification`.

## Findings and evidence connections

- **FinBooks:** S: light screenshots show a pale canvas, white sidebar/cards, dark text, spare green accents, consistent radii and clear monetary hierarchy. This makes primary actions legible without visual noise. R: `competitor_research/01_finbooks/ui_ux_analysis.md` describes dark-first styling, a dashboard carousel, mutually exclusive source/edit overlays and overlapping vendor headers. Light screenshot and dark-first research describe different observed states; use the captured light direction, avoid the documented review and table defects. Adopt its restraint, adapt its size/density for AP and avoid cloning brand identity.
- **Rossum:** S: source page and structured fields share the review viewport, with a clear document boundary, targeted highlighting, search and secondary drawers. R: `competitor_research/02_rossum/review_workspace_analysis.md` reports deeper document/field interactions. A still frame alone cannot establish synchronization, keyboard behavior or validation gating. Adapt the layout, not the dark palette.
- **Flick:** S: file views organize by category/period, the invoice builder has grouped inputs and inline validation, and dashboard charts can consume substantial space. R: `competitor_research/03_flickai/document_workspace_analysis.md` describes period-based workflows. The builder is sales-side; GST/TDS and several named views are unavailable or loading. Do not infer AP matching from these.
- **Cross-product and AP:** S: historical AP imagery shows invoice source, procurement matching, corrections, decisions and history, but small labels, repeated explanatory framing and busy status treatments compete with the decision. R: the cross-product evidence registers catalog the captures; their comparative claims and old policy values are not proof of current behavior. C: current `frontend/src/pages` and `Sidebar.tsx` show the current route/component structure; source is not a fresh runtime screenshot.

## Inaccessible or misleading evidence

All 206 PNG files were readable. Some images show loading, access-denied, empty or gated states; those underlying features remain inaccessible in the captured evidence, not necessarily broken in the products. The row annotations flag material caption mismatches, including FinBooks source viewer/exception, Rossum shortcut/confirmation/role views, Flick upload/files, and AP reset feedback. No missing named-file capture was invented. A missing Rossum numbered `07` review capture is not assumed to exist; screenshot numbering alone does not prove an omitted image. Other image formats, if later added, require a separate audit.
