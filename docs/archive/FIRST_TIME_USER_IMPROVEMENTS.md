# First-time user improvements

Priorities: **P1** prevents a novice misunderstanding the next step or financial meaning; **P2** improves orientation or scanability. Quick fixes are presentation and wording proposals; product/policy items need an owner to settle the underlying meaning or behavior before implementation. Evidence is the corresponding area in [observations](FIRST_TIME_USER_OBSERVATIONS.md).

| Area | Quick fix | Needs product or policy decision |
|---|---|---|
| Shell | **P1:** Explain “AP” once as accounts payable and label sign-off as an internal review, not a payment. **P2:** distinguish the product guide from the evaluation showcase and keep Help discoverable. | Decide whether role-specific navigation should explain unavailable sections and whether evaluator tools belong in a first-time finance view. |
| Today | **P1:** Define exception records, invoice liability and touchless rate beside their values; label the destination “View pending second approvals.” Explain that exception exposure and invoice total can be different measures. | Resolve the ready-for-approval versus pending-second-approval destination and the definition/source of each amount before changing counts or links. Confirm what unavailable means and how to recover it. |
| Inbox | **P1:** Add concise descriptions of each existing queue and say “internal approval recorded; payment is separate” where applicable. Expand PO and GSTIN on first use; explain that search applies to the displayed page if that remains true. | Agree which records belong in each queue, the meaning of “cleared,” and a safe approach to duplicate-looking records and missing extracted values. |
| Invoice review | **P1:** Replace “gates” in nearby explanatory copy with “checks”; explain the named failed checks and the next safe action in everyday language. Keep the document, all blockers, variances and prerequisites together. | Resolve blank-PDF viewing and define how to communicate verified document-unavailable states across browsers. Confirm which actions and corrections each role may take and their consequences. |
| Reconciliation | **P1:** Introduce “compare the bill with what was ordered and received,” define PO, GRN and QC inline, and explain variance as a difference. Keep original quantities, rates, source references and tolerance visible. | Agree when three-way versus four-way terminology applies, how to describe missing inspection evidence, and what financial meaning the suggested debit note has. |
| Exceptions | **P1:** Explain that each row is an issue record and an invoice can have multiple issues; define exposure and say Resolve opens the invoice. Label missing deadline as unknown rather than safe. | Confirm what exposure includes and whether owners, urgency and suggested next steps can be supported by existing data without inventing workflow state. Preserve reassignment and role checks. |
| Approvals | **P1:** Explain first and second reviewer, why they must differ, and that internal sign-off does not send money; keep the returned eligibility reason visible. | Confirm policy wording and threshold source for each organization; verify blocked and eligible Controller states with a real pending record before rollout. |
| Audit | **P1:** Describe this as the latest 100 requested events, explain actor and event reason, and make Details and horizontal scrolling easy to find. Keep raw source, before/after, reason and identifiers accessible. | Decide how a user retrieves older events and what an export covers; do not call this view or export a complete ledger without evidence. |
| Connections | **P1:** Begin each integration with “connected / not connected / unknown,” impact and the available next step; expand technical terms in administrator details. Do not infer status while loading. | Establish truthful readiness definitions and which role can authorize or import. Clarify live ERP versus snapshot capabilities. |
| Procurement records | **P1:** Explain each record type and source as “evaluation snapshot” or actual returned source; define order balance and why records are read-only. Keep provenance visible. | Confirm freshness, calculation basis and whether navigation from a record back to affected invoices is supported. |
| Team & Access | **P1:** Give each role a plain-language summary and explain who can approve access or invite someone; make the permissions matrix discoverable. | Validate role descriptions and threshold against effective server permissions; decide what identity details each role needs to see. |
| Setup | **P1:** Replace “You're Ready to Operate” with neutral checklist wording when prerequisites are not confirmed; explain “not confirmed” and avoid raw route names. | Decide which setup steps are required, optional or role-specific and which checks demonstrate actual readiness. Do not equate skipped with connected. |
| Welcome | **P1:** Define invoice, supplier and matching in one short example; expand PO, GRN and QC at first mention. Keep the live-data/no-decision notice prominent. | Decide whether a safe practice workspace exists; do not call live records a practice copy. Clarify whether each dismissal is temporary or persistent. |
| Guided tour | **P1:** Replace unexplained jargon with a task and a one-sentence definition per step; distinguish “still loading” from an unavailable target. Keep exit and restart visible. | Decide whether to offer a truly isolated practice record and verify role, no-invoice and failure routes before claiming coverage. Never submit decisions during the tour. |
| Contextual help | **P1:** Put short definitions and consequences beside unfamiliar controls, with Help for more detail; review each recommendation against the current screen and role. | Assign ownership for keeping help synchronized with policy and product behavior; validate wording with a first-time finance user. |

Visual defects observed in the walkthrough were corrected separately: setup button contrast, management count chips, the welcome/help surfaces, and non-semantic purple shell accents. Verify remaining states and contrast with real users and representative data before broader changes.
